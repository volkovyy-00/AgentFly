### What was built (all under `ui/src/trail/`, plus small edits in `App.tsx` and `graph/`)
- `?view=trail` shows the trail; any other URL is the unchanged lane graph. Mock: `?view=trail&mock=1&len=500` (`burst=N` to stream, `rich=1` adds rule text, messages and highlights).
- Trail: every step has a text badge (BLOCKED / WARNED / HIGHLIGHT chips; routine and secret-read rows carry a small "allowed"). Routine allowed runs fold into one row ("11 allowed steps · 5 read · 2 edit"). Blocks, warnings, highlights and secret reads never fold, and repeated identical blocks stay separate rows. Idle blocks are tinted, only the selected one is solid red. The rail runs from the marking step to the selected block. Pinned strip: "38 blocked · 38 of 38", Prev/Next, keys `n` / `p`.
- Follow: on by default, keeps the newest step in view, turns off when you scroll up or pick a row. While on, the newest block is selected as it arrives.
- Why card: rule id, trigger step -> blocked step (large box, dashed lines to host and rule), `agentfly rules draft …` / `check` / `sudo agentfly rules apply <draft>`, no allow button. Rule text and both messages only when the step carries them; otherwise it says the recorder did not send them. "Contents not recorded" sits in each step box. Warnings and highlights open the same card in their own colour, with no trigger.
- Hide/resize (the owner's choice, option A): Hide button or key `w` collapses the card to a 40 px bar ("Why card · R1 · Step 495", Show). A splitter resizes it: drag, arrow keys (24 px), double-click for the default. Stacked and side-by-side sizes are remembered separately in `localStorage`. The card keeps at least 160 px (280 wide) and the trail at least 240 px.
- Health strip: green dot and "Recorder up"; a big red OFFLINE banner plus a red OFFLINE in the header when the steps request fails (last steps stay). `last_hook_at` and `offline_guard_count` show only when `/health` sends them.

### Findings from the plan review that changed the build
1. `GET /api/steps?all=1` has no `marked_order` and `?limit=` is capped at 50 (`recorder/memory.py:16`, `:162-166`). So the page sends two requests per poll (`?all=1` and `?limit=1`) and uses the marker only if both name the same session. With no marker there is no rail and the card says the trigger is not known. It never guesses from `sensitive`.
2. `highlighted` is a verdict value (SPEC §4), not a flag. `Verdict`, `parseSnapshot` and the lane graph's `VERDICT_UI` know it now; the lane graph draws it as a plain step with a HIGHLIGHT chip.

### Choices I made that the owner may want to change
- Step numbers are the real `order` ("Step 495"). The trail mock starts at 1 like real sessions.
- Hiding the card is respected: a new block while hidden only updates the bar label. This means a block does not "open" the card for someone who hid it.
- Trigger step exists only for an R1 block. Other rules show no trigger and no marker is invented.
- No tabs in the header (History, Rules, Graph come with V9 and the home switch).
- Mock layout at 700 px: the pinned strip is three rows when a session has highlights (two otherwise), so the trail list is about 270 px tall at 800 px window height.
